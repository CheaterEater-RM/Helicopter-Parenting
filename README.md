# Helicopter Parenting

Children in RimWorld have a talent for learning in the worst possible places — drawing on the freezer
floor, jogging off to the map edge as a raid lands, or lying down to stargaze in a blizzard.
Helicopter Parenting steps in and steers them somewhere safer, without changing anything else about
how learning works.

## What it does

- **Floor drawing** — kids stop drawing in freezers and other dangerously cold or hot rooms. They'll
  pick a comfortable room instead (and tend to draw where other colonists are, like the living room).
  If nowhere better exists, they still draw where vanilla would have.
- **Nature running** — kids keep their distance from the map edge, and won't head out for a jog while
  there's an active threat on the map.
- **Sky dreaming** — kids won't lie down to stargaze in bad weather (blizzards, toxic fallout, extreme
  heat), and stay away from the map edge.

When the mod can't find a safer spot for an outdoor activity, the child simply does a different lesson
instead — they're never sent somewhere worse than the game would have picked.

## Settings

Everything is configurable in the mod options:

- Turn each of the three fixes on or off individually.
- Set how far from the map edge outdoor activities must stay.
- Block outdoor activities during raids and other threats (on by default).
- Optionally avoid nearby predators — off by default, since dealing with predators is part of the
  game. When on, kids also get up and leave a sky dream if a predator wanders too close.

## Requirements

- RimWorld 1.6 or 1.7
- Biotech
- Harmony

## Compatibility

Safe to add to or remove from any save at any time — the mod stores nothing in your save file. It only
affects the three activities above, so it plays nicely with most other mods; if another mod completely
overhauls one of those activities, Helicopter Parenting just steps aside for it.
